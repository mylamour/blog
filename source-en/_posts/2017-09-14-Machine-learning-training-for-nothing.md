# Preface

This post has [no useful content](https://img.iami.xyz/assets/ml_training.pptx), just complaints and rambling.

I've been organizing tech sharing sessions at my company for a while now. With good intentions, wanting to share knowledge and help everyone level up. So last Thursday, I gave a machine learning class to a bunch of my colleagues. The topic was set two weeks prior, and I spent 4-5 days preparing, hoping to make it as accessible and basic as possible to spark their interest and get them started. But how could things go as smoothly as I imagined? That's when I realized how much effort is needed behind anything that looks normal.

At my previous company, tech sharing was normal. Everyone was happy to participate, would focus during the session, and everyone was eager to improve. Here? A thousand kinds of reluctance. People playing on their phones, pretending to sleep. 
What a fucking mess.


# Content

**[Cost]**:
5 days - 2 full days, plus 3 hours per day for 3 days.

> Two datasets:

* Mnist 
* SMS  

> Three algorithms:

* KNN
* SVM
* Naive Bayes

> Two implementations:

* Scikit-Learn
* Tensorflow

> Other stuff

* Registered a domain name
* Deployed a chat room for pre/post discussion
* Deployed Jupyter for them to use and run online examples
* Printed materials, provided 4 days in advance.


I prepared two examples: `mnist` handwritten digit recognition and `SMS` spam text classification. Used the simplest algorithms: `KNN/SVM` (Mnist), NaiveBayes (SMS text), and implemented both `tensorflow` and `scikit-learn` versions. Mainly taught two algorithms: `KNN` and `Bayes`.


**[Gains]**:
>
* Deepened my understanding of these concepts and algorithms. Teaching others really does improve your own understanding.
* Probably pissed off a few people, but I don't care at all. If we were on the same level of understanding, this wouldn't have happened.
* Made the longest PPT from college until now. Realized how stupid and shallow I am.
* To achieve anything big, need to work harder, harder, and even harder.
